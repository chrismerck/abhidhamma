Haefliger knot tapestry

Each cell is the group of isotopy classes of smooth embeddings Sk → Sn (codimension q = n − k). Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, hover a cell for its value, status and source. Complete for k ≤ 13; beyond that only general theorems are filled in and everything else is marked unknown.
